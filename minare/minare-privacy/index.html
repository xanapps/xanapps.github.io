Privacy Policy

Minare — Prayer Times, Adhan, Dhikr Counter & Missed Prayer Tracking · Last updated: September 21, 2026
Summary (in short): Minare uses your location to calculate prayer times; this data is processed only on your device. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. You don't need to create an account.

Data We Collect

Minare uses the following information:

  • Location data: Used to calculate accurate prayer times and the qibla direction for your location. This data is processed only on your device and is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Missed-prayer records, dhikr/tasbih counters, and app settings (theme, notification preferences) that you add are stored only on your device.

Notifications

Minare uses your phone's local notification system to remind you of prayer times. These notifications are created and shown on your device.

Advertising

Minare shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data, such as device identifiers, in order to serve personalized ads. You can learn more about how this data is used in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

Your personal data is not shared with or sold to any third party other than what is described above (AdMob).

Children's Privacy

Minare is intended for a general audience and does not specifically aim to collect information from children.

Control Over Your Data

When you uninstall the app, all data we keep on your device is deleted as well.

Changes to This Policy

If we add new features in the future, we will update this policy and note the changes here.

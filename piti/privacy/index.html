PiTi

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026
PiTi is a workout and nutrition tracking app. No account is needed; everything you enter is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Data We Collect

PiTi itself does not collect any personal data that identifies you and does not send it to any server. There is no account, sign-in, analytics tool or crash-reporting tool. Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob; the data Google may process is described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

What you enter in the app is kept in your device's local storage:

  • Profile: your (optional) name, age, sex, height, weight, target weight, experience level, goal, equipment, pain/limitations, special conditions and PAR-Q health questionnaire answers
  • Your training program and history: sets, weights, reps, records, notes
  • Body measurements, fitness test results, food log, water, sleep, readiness and habit entries
  • Progress photos and form videos (in the app's own folder)
  • Preferences (language, theme, units, reminders), badges and your weekly day-pass counter

This health-related information is processed only on your device; it is not shared with us, ad networks or any other third party. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete it any time in Settings → Data → Delete all my data (except the weekly pass counter, which is deleted when you uninstall the app).

Permissions

Permissions are requested only when you use the related feature, and all are optional:

  • Camera: to scan food barcodes and record form videos
  • Microphone: to record sound while recording a form video
  • Photos: to pick a progress photo from your gallery
  • Notifications: for workout, water, sleep and weigh-in reminders and the rest timer

Location, contacts and Apple Health / Google Fit data are not accessed.

Network Use

  • Exercise animations and thumbnails are downloaded from our hosting server. This is an ordinary file request; the hosting provider (Cloudflare) may technically process your IP address for a short time. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • When you scan a food barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to fetch nutrition values. Open Food Facts privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
  • When you export your data (CSV) or share a report, the file goes only to the app you choose.
  • The internet is used to load ads (see below).

Ads (Google AdMob)

PiTi is free and shows banner, rewarded (optional — to earn a pass that unlocks locked sections for a day) and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Google may process data such as your device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Your workout, body and nutrition information is never sent to ad networks.

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ad Consent (GDPR)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (UMP). You can allow or refuse personalized ads; if you refuse, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the privacy options in the app's Settings.

iOS Tracking Permission (ATT)

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission is requested before the advertising ID (IDFA) can be used. If you don't allow it, the IDFA is not used and only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Device Advertising ID

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS the advertising ID is not shared with the app unless you allow tracking.

Health Notice

PiTi is not a medical device; programs, calorie/macro targets and suggestions are for general information only. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor before starting exercise or a diet.

Children

PiTi is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

Because your data lives only on your device, accessing, exporting (Settings → Data → CSV) and deleting it is entirely in your hands. We hold no records about you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

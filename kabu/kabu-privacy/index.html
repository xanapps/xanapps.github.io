Privacy Policy

KABU — Quit Bad Habits App · Last updated: July 28, 2026
Summary (in short): KABU stores your data only on your own phone. None of your information is sent to a server, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. You don't need to create an account.

Data We Collect

KABU stores the following information only on your phone, on-device:

  • The habits you add (name, type, day count, goal, daily cost)
  • Notes and Inspiration Board entries you write
  • Your app settings (theme, notification time)
  • If you enter it yourself: your emergency contact's name and phone number
  • If you enable the app lock: the PIN code you set

This data is never sent to our servers or any third party's servers. The current version has no cloud backup or account system — data is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted as well.

Notifications

KABU uses your phone's local notification system to send reminders at the time you set. These notifications are created and shown on your device; they are not sent through any server.

Phone Calls

On the "I'm struggling" screen, a link that opens your phone's dialer is used so you can call an emergency contact you've set with a single tap. KABU does not access your contacts; it only uses the number you manually entered in Settings.

Third-Party Services

KABU does not use any advertising network, analytics software, or tracking system. No ads are shown within the app.

Data Security

If you enable the app lock (PIN) feature, this code is stored only on your device and is used to restrict access to the app. This is a device-level protection; your PIN is never seen or stored by us.

Children's Privacy

KABU is a habit-tracking app intended for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. Since the app may involve personal goals related to sensitive categories (such as smoking or alcohol), it may not be suitable for pre-adolescent users.

Control Over Your Data

Since all data lives on your device:

  • When you delete a habit, that data is permanently removed
  • When you uninstall the app, all data is cleared from your device
  • You don't need to contact us to export or delete your data — you're always in full control

Changes to This Policy

If we add features such as cloud backup or an account system in the future, we will update this policy and note the changes here.

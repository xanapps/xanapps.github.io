Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026
OranLab is a statistics and odds analysis tool; it does not provide betting services. The app has no real-money betting, no payments and no links to betting sites.

Data Collected

OranLab itself does not collect personal data or send it to any server. There is no account or sign-in, and it requests no permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone. Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob, and Google may process the device data described below to show ads.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your app preferences (language, light/dark theme), your 18+ confirmation, your weekly report counter and your ad consent choices are kept in your device's local storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner, rewarded (optional) and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Google may process data such as the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse.

  • Betting and gambling ads are blocked.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ad Consent (GDPR)

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (UMP) when the app opens. You can allow or decline personalized ads; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Choices.

iOS Tracking Permission (ATT)

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission is requested before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Device Advertising ID

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS the advertising ID is not shared with the app unless you grant tracking permission.

Network Use

All statistics are calculated on your device from the historical match data bundled with the app; no internet is needed for the analyses. The internet is used only to load ads and for the Yeşilay link on the legal notice screen (if you tap it). There are no analytics tools or in-app purchases.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and on the app's Privacy Policy screen.
